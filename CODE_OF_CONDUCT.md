# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in MCP Sentinel a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, technically honest, security-conscious, and constructive. Accept responsibility, focus criticism on work, respect privacy, and avoid publishing exploit details before coordinated remediation.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, sexualized language, discriminatory conduct, doxxing, deliberate exposure of secrets, abuse of project infrastructure, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content, reject contributions, temporarily or permanently ban participation, and coordinate private security handling. Report conduct issues privately to the maintainers listed in repository security contacts. Maintainers must preserve reporter privacy and avoid conflicts of interest.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
